System Overview

The Hybrid Renewable Energy Simulation (HRES) project demonstrates how micro-generation assets can cooperate dynamically.

By resolving Solar, Wind, and Storage equations locally in the browser, users can instantly assess load compliance, cost impacts, and grid balances under custom scenarios.

Project Objectives

  • ▶
    Energy Management OptimizationImplement prioritizations to cover local demand using clean generation, avoiding peak grid tariffs.
  • ▶
    Dynamic Battery ModelingSimulate lithium-ion charge/discharge dynamics while imposing safety ceilings (capacity) and tracking State of Charge (SOC) metrics.
  • ▶
    Cost Comparison AnalysisProve economic benefits side-by-side using import/export pricing models (import at ₹8/kWh, export at ₹6/kWh).
  • ▶
    Environmental Footprint AssessmentQuantify total CO₂ footprint offsets daily utilizing localized carbon grid-replacement factors.
